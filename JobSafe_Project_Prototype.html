Paste the text of a job ad below. This demo uses transparent keyword rules only; it does not use a trained machine-learning model or verify a company online.

What this prototype looks for

Upfront paymentsRegistration, training, security, or application fees.
Unrealistic promisesVery high income claims with little experience or effort.
Urgency & pressurePressure to act immediately or guaranteed selection.
Sensitive informationRequests for OTPs, passwords, or bank details.

Safety reminder

A warning flag is not proof that a job is fraudulent, and no flags do not guarantee that a job is genuine. Never share OTPs or passwords, avoid paying to get a job, and confirm openings through the employer's official website or verified contact details.
